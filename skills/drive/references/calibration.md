# Calibration lessons

The bundled Limbic cases are behavioral regressions, not examples to imitate
word for word. Run them with `python -m limbic.drive calibrate`.

## NRK mobile and Apple TV

A broad request led to fourteen agents, thirteen hours, sixty-eight commits, and
hundreds of changed files. Later hands-on use produced important redirection and
exposed a crash. The useful loop was artifact, human reaction, correction.

Expected first move: one core journey on one platform, experienced by the user.

## Otak and the Hirsch investigation

Twenty-four Codex jobs generated strong research, then a Fable follow-up created
dozens of account writers and audits before the decisive ten-minute browsing test.

Expected first move: one or two disputed propositions in a navigable slice, then
the user's usefulness judgment.

## Codex and Claude workflow research

The report was useful, but public harness research preceded the closest local
evidence and the short actionable recommendation arrived after a long report.

Expected first move: inspect the local runs and compile a one-page direction card
before expanding the research.
